一个复杂产品进入不同市场后，真正需要改变的是什么？

What Really Needs to Change When a Complex Product Enters a New Market?

产品进入一个新市场以后，最容易提出的问题是：“这里需要做哪些本地化修改？”但如果把问题只理解为语言、界面、法规或功能调整，往往已经把真正需要判断的范围缩得太小。跨市场产品真正需要处理的，是哪些部分必须保持稳定，哪些部分必须重新理解，以及哪些看似属于产品的问题，其实来自市场、组织或运营条件的变化。

Once a product enters a new market, the first question is usually which local changes to make. If that question only means language, interface, regulation or features, the real scope of judgment has already been narrowed too far. A cross-market product has to decide what must stay stable, what has to be understood again, and which problems that look like product issues actually come from the market, the organization or the conditions of operation.

不是所有差异都应该变成产品差异

Not every difference belongs in the product

一个国家与另一个国家不同，并不意味着所有差异都应该被写进产品。市场之间的不同可以很多：气候、支付习惯、监管文本、渠道结构，以及用户对“好用”的理解，都可能不一样。如果每一种不一样都要变成一个功能、一套文案或一条配置，产品很快就会失去自己的形状。

更有用的起点，是把差异分开看。有些差异会改变用户是否还能得到原来承诺的价值。同样是到达一个地点，在道路编号清楚的地方和在地址体系不稳定的地方，用户要完成的任务可能已经不同。有些差异决定产品能不能合法地被使用，例如数据能不能用、告知要不要改、某些能力在当地是否被允许。还有一些差异主要影响交付和运营：谁来更新，出了问题谁响应，当地是否有人能把服务接住。剩下的一类，往往只是表面偏好。改了会更顺，不改也不妨碍产品成立。

这几类不需要一张放之四海的清单。需要的是在具体产品里把它们分开，而不是一看到“本地”两个字，就把所有差异都推进需求表。

One country differing from another does not mean every difference should be encoded into the product. Markets differ in climate, payment habits, regulatory text, channel structure and in what users treat as “good enough.” If each of those becomes a feature, a copy variant or a configuration, the product soon loses its shape.

A more useful start is to separate the differences. Some change whether users still receive the value that was promised. Getting to a place is not the same task where roads are clearly numbered and where the address system itself is unstable. Some differences decide whether the product can be used lawfully: whether data may be used, whether disclosure has to change, whether a capability is allowed. Others mainly affect delivery and operations: who updates the product, who answers when it fails, and whether anyone local can actually carry the service. What remains is often surface preference. Changing it makes the product smoother. Leaving it does not stop the product from working.

These categories do not need a universal checklist. They need to be separated inside a specific product, rather than pushed onto a requirements list the moment the word “local” appears.

真正需要调整的，常常不是功能

What changes is often not the feature

团队讨论跨市场时，容易把问题收成“要不要加一个本地功能”。功能当然会出现，但它经常只是更早判断的结果。目标用户可能已经变了。同一个产品，在一个市场服务职业车队，在另一个市场服务私人车主，后面的优先级就不会一样。使用场景可能变了。价值主张也可能需要重说：用户要的到底是更快到达，是更少走错，还是在陌生环境里仍然敢用。

数据条件会改写判断。一个市场有稳定、可更新的基础数据，另一个市场的数据稀疏、更新慢、口径也不一致。这时先改界面，往往解决不了用户碰到的问题。渠道、交付和服务同样会把看起来属于软件的问题带到软件外面。组织接口也是。总部以为这是一个版本配置，当地面对的却是另一套审批、另一批合作方和另一种上线节奏。

所以，产品适配不能只被理解成功能的本地化。功能是看得见的一层。前面还有用户、场景、价值、数据、渠道、交付、服务，以及谁和谁对接。

Cross-market discussions collapse easily into whether to add a local feature. Features do appear, but they are often the result of an earlier judgment. The user may already be different. The same product serving professional fleets in one market and private drivers in another will not keep the same priorities. The situation of use may change. The value proposition may need to be said again: is the user buying a faster arrival, fewer wrong turns, or the confidence to keep using the product in an unfamiliar place?

Data conditions rewrite the judgment. One market has stable base data that can be updated. Another has sparse data, slow updates and inconsistent definitions. Changing the interface first rarely fixes what the user actually meets. Channels, delivery and service pull problems that look like software out of the software. So does the organizational interface. Headquarters sees a version setting. The local team faces a different approval path, different partners and a different release rhythm.

Product adaptation is therefore not the same thing as feature localization. The feature is the visible layer. In front of it sit the user, the situation, the value, the data, the channel, delivery, service, and who has to work with whom.

必须留下一个稳定内核

A stable core still has to remain

如果每进入一个市场都重新做一套产品，短期看像是尊重差异，长期看企业并没有积累全球产品能力。下一次进入新市场，仍然要从头争论。版本之间难以复用测试，问题也难以对照着学。

因此需要持续区分三件事。有些部分必须全球一致，因为它们决定这个产品为什么是这个产品：核心任务、关键质量、基本的信息结构。有些部分应该做成可配置的，让差异被容纳，而不是被写成另一套产品。还有一些部分必须本地适配，因为不改，产品在那个市场里就不能用，或者用了会伤害用户。

这三层不会在第一天就分干净。分得太死，会把还没看懂的差异锁进“必须全球一致”。分得太松，每个市场都会变成例外。比较成熟的做法，是让这个区分可以被复查，而不是写成一份再也不动的原则。

Rebuilding the product for every new market can look like respect for difference. Over time it means the company never accumulates a global product capability. The next market starts the argument again. Tests are hard to reuse, and problems are hard to compare.

Three distinctions have to be kept alive. Some parts must stay globally consistent, because they are why the product is this product: the core task, the critical quality bar, the basic information structure. Some parts should be configurable, so difference is absorbed rather than turned into another product. Some parts must be adapted locally, because without the change the product cannot be used in that market, or using it would harm the user.

Those layers are not clean on day one. Drawn too tightly, they lock a misunderstood difference into “must be global.” Drawn too loosely, every market becomes an exception. A more mature practice is to make the distinction reviewable, not to freeze it as a principle that can never be reopened.

真正困难的是边界判断

The hard part is the boundary

跨市场产品管理里，最容易被简化的句子是“改，或者不改”。真实的工作没有这么整齐。需要回答的是：在哪里保持一致，在哪里允许差异，谁来做这个判断，以及根据什么证据再调整。

证据不一定是一张完整的报表。它可以是用户完不成任务的具体情形，可以是当地规则让某个流程走不通，也可以是交付团队反复用人工补上的那一段。没有这类证据，差异讨论很容易变成立场。总部说要统一，当地说这里特殊。立场可以打开对话，但不能代替判断。

真正成熟的全球产品，不是完全一样，也不是每个市场都完全不同。它需要一个稳定的产品内核，同时具备理解差异、配置差异和学习差异的能力。

The sentence that oversimplifies cross-market product work is “change it, or don’t.” The work is less tidy than that. The questions are where consistency holds, where difference is allowed, who makes that call, and what evidence would justify changing it later.

The evidence does not have to be a finished dashboard. It can be a concrete case of a user failing the task, a local rule that blocks a flow, or the manual patch a delivery team keeps applying. Without that kind of evidence, the discussion becomes a position. Headquarters says unify. The local team says this market is special. A position can open the conversation. It cannot replace the judgment.

A mature global product is neither identical everywhere nor wholly different in every market. It needs a stable core, and the ability to understand difference, configure difference and learn from difference.
