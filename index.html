01 / 关于About

在不同市场之间理解产品，在长期实践中形成判断

Understanding products across markets — and turning practice into judgment

我的工作长期处在全球产品、国际化与本地化、汽车软件的交叉地带。关注的不只是产品能否进入一个市场，更关注它能否在不同规则、文化、数据与组织条件下持续运行。

My work sits at the intersection of global products, internationalization and localization, and automotive software. I am interested not only in whether a product can enter a market, but whether it can continue to work under different rules, cultures, data conditions and organizational realities.

过去二十多年，我的职业实践持续围绕一个问题展开：一个复杂产品，如何跨越语言、文化、市场与组织边界，仍然保持可用、可运营、可规模化。

从全球科技产品的国际化与本地化，到数字业务与运营，再到汽车导航与地图产品管理，我逐步形成的不是单点经验，而是一种跨市场看产品的工作方式：把战略、用户、数据、规则、组织和交付放在同一个系统中判断。

写作让我把这些经验进一步结构化。《出海新势力：GoxEDGE全球拓展战略模型》是一次阶段性的系统整理；iSeeWorlds 是我的长期个人主页，记录职业实践、产品与技术观察、写作，以及那些值得持续追问的问题。

For more than two decades, my work has revolved around one recurring question: how can a complex product cross language, cultural, market and organizational boundaries while remaining usable, operable and scalable?

From internationalization and localization of global technology products, to digital business and operations, and later automotive navigation and maps, I developed a cross-market way of working that considers strategy, users, data, rules, organization and delivery as one system.

Writing is how I make that experience explicit. GoxEDGE is one structured expression of that work; iSeeWorlds is my long-term personal home for professional experience, observations on products and technology, writing, and questions worth continuing to explore.

从“走出去”到“走下去”

From entering overseas markets to building the capability to stay and operate

《出海新势力：GoxEDGE全球拓展战略模型》讨论的不是如何把同一个产品卖到更多国家，而是企业如何判断起点、选择市场、设计路径、形成能力，并在不断变化的规则、市场、文化与组织条件下真正“走下去”。

The book is not simply about selling the same product in more countries. It examines how companies assess their starting point, select markets, design an expansion path, build the required capabilities, and continue operating as rules, markets, cultures and organizational demands change.

GoxEDGE 全球拓展战略模型 The GoxEDGE Global Expansion Strategy Model 战略判断 · EDGE 四大能力 · 六阶段路径 · 认知—行动—反馈飞轮 Strategic judgment · Four EDGE capabilities · Six-stage path · Cognition–action–feedback flywheel

全书以战略判断为起点，通过 EDGE 四大能力、探索—定位—执行—赋能—优化—可持续六个阶段，以及认知—行动—反馈飞轮，把市场进入、产品适配、组织承接与能力学习连接为一套持续演进的全球拓展体系。

GoxEDGE treats global expansion as an evolving operating system rather than a one-time market-entry decision. It connects strategic judgment, four EDGE capabilities, a six-stage path from exploration to sustainability, and a cognition–action–feedback flywheel into one coherent model.
